The SwiftUI component used in rendering an image fetched using a URL is an "AsyncImage()". Generally, you may try to resize an image by applying frame modifiers, but this approach often leads to unexpected results. This article explores the proper techniques for resizing internet-fetched images in SwiftUI iOS development, covering best practices and performance considerations.
