Engineered a comprehensive **Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE)** service that functions as both peripheral and central device, enabling seamless IoT device connectivity and provisioning via smartphones. The system supports both **Android and iOS** platforms, ensuring broad compatibility for US customer base.

**Key Features:**
- Dual-mode BLE operation (peripheral & central)
- Cross-platform mobile app connectivity
- Secure device provisioning protocols
- Real-time device management capabilities

**Impact:** Streamlined IoT device onboarding process, reducing setup time by 70% and improving user experience for end customers.
